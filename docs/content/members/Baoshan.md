About Baoshan 

Baoshan Wu is a professional instrumental instructor. She is a well-qualified music educator with two Master Degrees of music performance and music education, graduated in the Art University Zurich, Switzerland. 

Baoshan offers excellence flute and piano tuitions to all age groups and individuals and flute ensembles. 

Baoshan was the president of flute society SA and adjudicator of Eisteddfod Adelaide in the past years. She always works with passion and inspires her students to enjoy the music performance.
