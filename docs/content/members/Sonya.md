Sonya Chong

Committee Member, South Australian International Musicians Association.

Sonia Chong is a Russian-Chinese singer born in Ili, Xinjiang, China. Known for her warm and distinctive vocal style, she has been widely recognised as the “Teresa Teng of the Western Regions.”

She is a member of the Xinjiang Musicians Association and the Xinjiang Popular Music Association, and serves as Vice President of the Ili Prefecture Recitation Association.

Sonia has appeared on several major Chinese television programs, including CCTV-3’s *Very 6+1*, *Golden 100 Seconds* and *The Happiness Bill*. She was also a featured guest on Shanxi Television’s *Songs from the Yellow River*. In 2023, she was invited to participate in the tenth-anniversary celebration of *Golden 100 Seconds*.

Her achievements include placing among the top three performers in the China Farmers’ Song Competition and receiving the National Most Beautiful Ethnic Voice Award. She has also been invited to perform in the Anhui Television Spring Festival Gala, the National Rural Spring Festival Gala and the National Performing Arts Spring Festival Gala.

Internationally, Sonia has held four solo concerts in Australia and a solo concert in Shanghai. She has also participated in the inaugural International Chinese New Year Gala in Sydney and the Huaxing Chinese New Year Gala in Melbourne.

In recognition of her contribution to cultural exchange, Sonia was awarded the honorary title of *China–Australia Cultural Ambassador.**

A distinctive multicultural performer, Sonia is highly regarded by audiences from diverse cultural and ethnic backgrounds. She is able to sing in several languages and has recorded and performed nearly one hundred original songs, many of which celebrate the landscapes, cultures and communities of her homeland.
