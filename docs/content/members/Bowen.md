Guqin Performer | Interdisciplinary Arts Practitioner | Music and Arts Wellbeing Practice

Bowen Huang is a guqin performer based in Adelaide, Australia, with an interdisciplinary academic background in Applied Psychology and Electronic Engineering.

He has devoted himself to the long-term study and practice of the traditional Chinese guqin, exploring its rich aesthetic traditions, humanistic philosophy, and contemporary relevance.

In addition to his musical practice, Bowen holds Master’s degrees in both Applied Psychology and Electronic Engineering. His interdisciplinary background informs an artistic practice that connects music, psychology, technology, and culture, with particular interests in emotional wellbeing, attention development, and the therapeutic potential of music and the arts. With over ten years of experience in guqin teaching and artistic practice, he is committed to exploring the role of traditional music in cultural engagement, personal wellbeing, and cross-cultural dialogue.

As a member of the South Australia International Music Association (SAIMA), Bowen actively contributes through performances, education, cultural exchange, and community projects, promoting the guqin within an international context while fostering intercultural understanding and artistic dialogue.
