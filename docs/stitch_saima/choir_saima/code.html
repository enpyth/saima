About the Program

Our choir is more than just a musical ensemble; it's a sanctuary for cultural preservation and emotional bonding. We focus on the values of active participation, mutual respect, and the deep connection fostered when parent and child share a microphone.

"Music is the shortest distance between two hearts."

First Steps

Our journey began with a magnificent debut at the Jiangsu Chamber of Commerce annual performance. Witnessing our families take the stage for the first time was a testament to the power of community music.

"The energy was electric. Seeing the pride in the children's eyes while standing beside their parents was the exact vision SAIMA had for this program."

— Program Director
